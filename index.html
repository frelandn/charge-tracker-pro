📖 Charge Tracker Pro User Guide

🚀 1. System Configurations

Tap ⚙️ System Configurations on the Ride Log tab to customize:

  • Battery Setup: Choose 1 or 2 Battery Packs.
  • Baseline ODO: Your starting odometer reading.
  • Charger & Rate: Input wattage and local ₱/kWh electricity rate.

🔋 2. Logging Rides & Recharges

Regular Ride: Enter ending battery % for Pack A/B and Current ODO. Review calculated trip stats and tap 💾 Log Ride & Charge Event.

Full Recharge: Check ⚡ Full Charge Reset. Optionally enter actual meter kWh, then log. Baseline battery resets to 100% for next trip.

⚠️ 3. BMS Imbalance Alert

Appears if Pack A and Pack B differ by 5% or more. Perform a 100% full recharge to re-balance battery cells.

📊 4. History & Analytics

History Tab: Review aggregate stats, monthly breakdowns, and delete entries if needed.

Analytics Tab: View trend graphs for efficiency (%/km) and trip distances.

💾 5. Data Backup & Restore

Use 📂 Restore to load past backup files, 💾 Backup to save JSON database copies, or 📥 CSV for Excel spreadsheets.
